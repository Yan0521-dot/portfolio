Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aathityan's personal portfolio website handles information when visitors use the website and view embedded TikTok content.

1. Information We Handle

The portfolio is a publicly accessible website. When the TikTok integration is enabled, it may retrieve and display the account owner's basic TikTok profile information and public video information through TikTok's Display API.

This information may include a TikTok username, public video descriptions, video identifiers, cover images and embed information.

2. TikTok Account Authorization

The account owner may authorize the integration through TikTok Login Kit. Authorization is used to obtain permission to access the account information and public video list required by the portfolio.

Visitors to the portfolio do not need to sign in to TikTok or authorize access to their own accounts to view the portfolio's embedded videos.

3. Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens required for the integration are intended to be handled by a Cloudflare Worker and protected server-side storage. They must not be exposed in the public website's source code.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages and may use Cloudflare to operate its server-side integration. TikTok videos are embedded using TikTok services.

When visitors load or interact with embedded TikTok content, TikTok may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. Visitors should review TikTok's policies for details about its data practices.

5. Cookies and Usage Data

GitHub Pages, Cloudflare and embedded third-party services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request details and security logs as part of providing and protecting their services. TikTok embeds may use cookies or similar technologies subject to TikTok's own policies and visitor settings.

6. Data Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally sold by this portfolio. Data obtained through TikTok is used only for the portfolio integration and its operation, subject to applicable platform requirements.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the integration, maintain security and meet applicable requirements. The account owner may revoke authorization through TikTok. For privacy questions or requests relating to information handled by this portfolio, contact the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Visitors should not submit sensitive personal information through the website.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website or its integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
