GET TO KNOW ME

Beyond the Code.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Information Technology (Honours), majoring in Computer Forensics at Universiti Tun Abdul Razak (UNIRAZAK). My interests include cybersecurity, digital investigations, penetration testing, Linux, AI development and software engineering.

I enjoy exploring how systems work, understanding how vulnerabilities happen, and developing solutions that make technology more useful and secure. Beyond conventional technology, I'm also fascinated by space technologies, particularly rockets, space exploration and the role of cybersecurity in protecting space-based systems and infrastructure.

Outside academics, I'm actively involved in the Malaysian cybersecurity community through HackDev as a BOD member, contributing to community initiatives, events and knowledge-sharing activities.

I'm also the founder of Mom's Perisa , a SaaS platform currently under development that aims to connect customers with home cooks and help home-based food businesses reach more customers online.

While existing food delivery platforms already offer home-cooked food options, many home cooks may not be aware of these opportunities or have an accessible way to build their own online presence. Mom's Perisa aims to address this gap through a more affordable and efficient platform.

Planned differentiators include lower platform costs and monthly chef-sharing sessions where home cooks can learn, exchange experiences and improve their businesses. The platform is still in its development phase, with the long-term goal of turning it into a business.
